Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Application: artKoala Studio Ops

1. Purpose of This Application

artKoala Studio Ops is a private, single-owner publishing, comment-management, and analytics integration operated exclusively by the administrator of the official artKoala social media accounts (including facebook.com/artKoalaaa). The application is not offered to the general public and does not provide third-party user login or consumer accounts.

2. Information Accessed

Through official Meta APIs, the application accesses only aggregate Page/account insights, media publishing endpoints, and public comments posted directly on the artKoala Page/Instagram account so the Page administrator can publish creative media, read aggregated performance metrics, and respond to public comments.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Publishing and scheduling original creative posts, Reels, and Stories to artKoala's own accounts.
  • Reading aggregated engagement and reach insights for internal performance review.
  • Reviewing and replying to public comments left on artKoala's own posts.

4. No Sale or Third-Party Sharing

artKoala Studio Ops does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers, and does not collect private personal profiles from visitors.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Only minimal operational logs (aggregated post metrics and publication records) are stored locally by the Page administrator. For data deletion instructions, see our User Data Deletion Instructions.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the artKoala Page administrator directly via official Page messaging at https://www.facebook.com/artKoalaaa/.
