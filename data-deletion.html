User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Application: artKoala Studio Ops

artKoala Studio Ops is an internal single-owner publishing and analytics tool for the official artKoala Page. It does not offer third-party logins and does not store personal user profiles or private account databases outside Meta.

How to Request Removal of Any Referenced Interaction

  1. If you have interacted publicly with the artKoala Facebook Page or Instagram account (for example, by posting a public comment), you may delete your comment directly at any time inside Facebook or Instagram.
  2. If you would like the artKoala administrator to remove any local reference or reply associated with your public interaction, send a direct message to https://www.facebook.com/artKoalaaa/ with the link or date of the post and your public handle.
  3. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 days and confirmed via reply message.
